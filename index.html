who's this?

Hi, I'm Minh, but most people call me Nora. I studied business, yet I've always been curious about two things: how technology works and how people think. Product design is where the two meet, so that's where I live now.

I think good tech should feel invisible. My favourite job is taking a scary, complicated system and making it feel obvious, whether that's AI agents, taxes or a hospital form.
