Jan 2025 / UX research / Concept study

What if Zalo was our neighborhood market?

A 3-month UX project with Mirr Design: how apartment residents in Vietnam buy, sell and swap things in Zalo groups, and how we made it easier, right inside the chat.

timeline
Oct 2024 to Jan 2025
role
UX/UI designer in training
skills
UX research, testing, wireframing
team
2 designers, mentored by Mirr Design

01 the challenge

70 million users, and one very messy group chat

Zalo has 70+ million users. That's a huge chance for shop owners, local communities and small businesses to connect and trade. But anyone who has lived in an apartment building knows the group chat: hundreds of messages a day, blurry photos, and no real way to follow up on that rice cooker someone posted at 7am.

So we asked: how can we change the way people in residential areas buy and sell, with a smooth peer-to-peer experience inside the Zalo groups they already trust?

Busy parents

Small business owners

25 to 50

  • Zalo groups are mostly for building updates and announcements.
  • People struggle to find what they need in the chat.
  • Trust in product quality and sellers is low.
  • The current interface isn't built for buying and selling.
  • There is real potential for Zalo to become an internal marketplace.
  • People want seller ratings, transparent payment, order tracking, reviews and safe handling of personal data.

05 persona

Meet Thùy Nhiên

Nhiên is 28, works in an office and lives in a busy apartment complex. She loves hidden gems and good deals. Her building's Zalo group was meant for community news, but it has turned into a chaotic free-for-all.

One morning a neighbor posted a secondhand item she really wanted. An hour later she went back to find it and got lost scrolling. Gone. She wants the chat to feel less like a source of anxiety and more like a simple marketplace with her neighbors.

“I'm tired of scrolling through countless messages and messy images just to find what I need. I want a simple, reliable way to shop in my community, at a fair price, without worrying about the risks.”

Thùy Nhiên, our persona

06 problems to ideas

Three problems, three directions

problemClunky shopping. Finding products, comparing prices and closing a deal in a group chat is a pain.
how might weMake discovery, talking to sellers and paying feel smooth inside Zalo groups?
ideaBetter seller chat, smart search, and a quick checkout with order tracking.
problemThin product info. One photo and a line of text is not enough.
how might weGive buyers complete, trustworthy product information?
ideaFull product pages with images, video, reviews and proof of origin.
problemLow trust. Buyers worry about fakes, poor quality and scams.
how might weMake sellers and products credible and transparent?
ideaRatings, seller verification, buyer protection and community feedback.

07 structure

Card sorting and user flows

Five main flows

  • 1. Search for products in messages or the group marketplace
  • 2a. Buy any product
  • 2b. Make a payment
  • 3. Create or view thread messages
  • 4a. View a seller's profile
  • 4b. Chat with a seller
  • 5. Review and rate a purchased order

10 usability testing

What testing taught us

Trust and security

Stronger user protection policies, and an interface that inspires confidence.

Product information

A "Call" button on product pages, like Chợ Tốt. Simpler filters.

Intuitive interface

A clearer "Buy" icon than the cart. Consistent star ratings.

Clear, friendly content

Easy, welcoming copy. Clear wording on who handles shipping.
