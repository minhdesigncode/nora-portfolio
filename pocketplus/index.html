01 the moment

Imagine this

You're at the checkout. The cashier has scanned everything, you reach for your wallet, and your card... is gone. Panic.

Or you're at the airport. The check-in line is long, you reach for your boarding pass, and your heart skips a beat. Not there.

These aren't tiny inconveniences. Pocketplus+ is designed to free you from moments like these.

02 overview

One pocket for everything you carry

Pocketplus+ is a wallet app for keeping personal cards and important documents on your phone, built for urban Vietnamese users. Bank cards, ID, tickets: one secure place, so a forgotten wallet no longer ruins your day.

Our goal: an intuitive app that lets people manage their digital stuff easily, without carrying a physical wallet or worrying about losing it.

04 competitors

What Apple and Google already do well

Apple Wallet

  • Stores credit cards, boarding passes, event tickets and membership cards
  • Popular where iPhones dominate: the US, Canada, Japan, Australia, parts of Europe
  • High security with Face ID or Touch ID

Google Wallet

  • Stores credit and debit cards, gift cards and event tickets
  • Favored in Android-heavy markets across Asia, South America and Europe
  • Fingerprint and PIN security

So we wondered: what if there was a wallet as convenient as Apple and Google Wallet, but designed for Vietnamese people, their local needs and a wider range of public and private uses?

06 the problem

The real problem

People who often forget their ID, membership cards, tickets or bank details need one central, easy-to-reach place for all of it. Keeping documents in random apps or a cluttered photo library makes them hard to find, which means frustration and wasted time.

08 visual system

A playful wallet, built on a shared system

Four card colors from the brand (green, blue, orange and yellow) carry through a style guide, type scale, icon library and a simple component guide, so the whole team designs the same way. I led a team with graphic designers and people from other backgrounds, so a shared system really mattered.

11 feedback

What users told us

  • Logo: the "add card" icon felt a bit too similar to Timo's.
  • Bank card flow: adding a card works well.
  • ID card flow: two screens had an unclear purpose. Card details should move to their own flow.
  • Empty cards: a card with no bank details yet should look blank.
  • Card details: tapping a card should open details with copy actions.
  • Security: hide transaction history behind an extra step, in case you show your card to someone.
