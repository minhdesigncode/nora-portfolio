Making tax settings a little less scary

How we turned DynaTax's tangled settings into two clear levels, you and your business, for a US platform that connects small business owners with freelance tax pros.

client
DynaTax AI, US taxtech
role
Product designer (freelance)
team

Settings, billing, onboarding

01 what is dynatax

Bookkeeping, taxes and a community, all in one place

DynaTax handles accounting and tax work for small businesses in the US. It's also a community: business owners, from truckers to nail salon owners and freelancers, meet freelance CPAs and tax pros who can help them.

Business owner

Runs one or more businesses, keeps the books IRS-ready, finds a tax pro when needed.

Tax pro

A freelance CPA or tax professional who manages clients and builds a reputation in the community.

02 the challenge

We inherited a fully vibe-coded product

The DynaTax dev team had built the whole platform with AI, from the founder's idea and AI-generated docs. It worked! But nobody had designed it. Our job was to turn it into a web product people can actually use, and our epic was Settings.

The biggest knot: settings mixed two different levels, the person and the business. Organization settings and account settings sat side by side in one tab bar.

Two levels, one page

Profile, organizations, billing and community shared one tab bar. What applies to you, and what to your business?

Many businesses, no home

Owners with an LLC, a 1099 side gig and a trucking company had nowhere to switch, compare or set a default.

Hidden blockers

Plan limits, missing entity details and unverified identity quietly blocked tax filing.

03 research

Borrowing good ideas from TaxDome, Xero and QuickBooks

We studied tax and accounting platforms like TaxDome, Xero and QuickBooks, plus tax communities such as Reddit's tax forums. Xero became our main reference: it's very close to DynaTax and handles the split between organization and account settings especially well.

04 information architecture

One question: is this about you, or your business?

Once the IA was agreed, we split settings in two. Anything about you lives under your avatar, bottom left. Anything about the business lives top right, next to the business switcher. Two doors, no guessing.

Account settings, from your avatar

  • Personal information: name, contact, address, tax info
  • Business management: all your businesses
  • Billing and invoices, across businesses
  • Account verification: email, phone, identity
  • Account security: password, 2FA
  • Notifications: SMS, digest, quiet hours
  • Community alias: how you appear

Business settings, from the switcher

  • Organization overview: name, logo, community profile
  • Industry: trucker, small business, freelancer
  • Pricing plan and subscription
  • Usage meters: AI queries, documents, storage
  • Payment methods and invoices
  • Team members and roles

05 onboarding & industry

A trucker and a nail salon should not get the same app

During registration, a short survey asks what kind of business you run, like trucking, a nail salon or freelance work. The answer sets the industry, and the industry decides the features and layout you get: per diem and IFTA for truckers, invoicing for small businesses, quarterly estimates for 1099 contractors.

06 account settings

Everything about you, one click from your avatar

Account menu

A slide-out menu shows your status at a glance, before you open a single page.

  • Business count under business management
  • "Not verified" right under verification

Personal information

Verification sits next to the data it checks, and the community alias is set right here.

  • Inline "Verify now" per field
  • Tax info: SSN, filing status, dependents

Verify in place

A 6-digit code confirms email or phone without leaving the page.

  • Resend with a 30s timer
  • One clear primary action

Business management

Every business you own or belong to, with role, owner, members and industry. Problems show up inline, each with one fix.

  • Plan limit, upgrade plan
  • Missing entity, set up entity
  • Set a default business

All billing

One table for every subscription across all your businesses.

  • Status chips: due soon, processing, paid
  • View detail, change plan, cancel

Safe logout

Logging out in the middle of a bank sync or an AI report could lose data, so waiting is the default.

  • Primary: wait and close
  • Secondary: log out anyway

07 business settings

Plans, usage and payments, for each business

The current plan, what it includes and how much is left, all in one view. Usage meters turn yellow, then red, before you hit a limit, not after.

08 design system

Built on DynaTax UI Kit 2.0

Every screen uses the shared UI kit we maintained with reLab: global color tokens, an Inter type scale, button states and data components like metric cards.

09 impact

So, what changed?

A clear mental model

"Me" lives under the avatar, "my business" lives top right. No more guessing which level a setting belongs to.

Fewer blind spots

Plan limits, missing entity data and verification show up before they block tax work.

Ready to scale

Industry-based layouts and reusable components let DynaTax add new segments without redesigning settings.
